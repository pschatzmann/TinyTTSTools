 One packed phoneme symbol's canonical Huffman code, for the widened uint16_t packing CompactPhonemeDictionaryBuilder.h's Seg() uses: (phone_id << 2) | stress, with a non-stress modifier OR'd in at bit 9 (see Seg()'s own doc).
